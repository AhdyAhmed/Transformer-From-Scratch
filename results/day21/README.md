# Day 21 — TensorFlow CPU subset run

This directory contains the lightweight reproducibility artifacts from the
verified Day 21 TensorFlow run. TensorFlow model and optimizer checkpoints
remain under `checkpoints/day21-medium/tf/` and are intentionally ignored by
Git.

## Configuration

- Framework: TensorFlow 2.21 / Keras 3.13
- Device: native Windows CPU (`/device:CPU:0`; no TensorFlow GPU visible)
- Dataset: first 5,000 Multi30k training pairs and first 500 validation pairs
- Tokenizer: joint English/German from-scratch BPE, 512 tokens, selected training data only
- Model: 64 dimensions, 4 heads, 2 encoder and decoder layers, 256-dimensional FFN
- Parameters: 299,776
- Batch size: 64
- Maximum sequence length: 48 (144 training pairs and 16 validation pairs truncated)
- Epochs: 10 (epoch 10 resumed from the epoch-9 latest checkpoint)
- Optimizer steps: 790

## Results

| Epoch | Step | Train loss | Validation loss | Train tokens/s |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 79 | 4.8390 | 4.1279 | 1,542 |
| 2 | 158 | 3.8790 | 3.8500 | 1,386 |
| 5 | 395 | 3.5340 | 3.6506 | 841 |
| 9 | 711 | 3.3868 | 3.5175 | 837 |
| 10 | 790 | **3.3631** | **3.4998** | 834 |

Training loss fell by 30.5% and validation loss by 15.2%. Both series remained
finite, epoch 10 became the best checkpoint, and the independent resume process
restored epoch 9 and optimizer step 711 before continuing to step 790.

The greedy samples become more structured but remain generic and repetitive.
This is expected from the deliberately CPU-bounded subset, compact model, and
512-token vocabulary. The run verifies TensorFlow correctness and learning;
formal cross-framework quality evaluation remains scheduled for Day 23.

Artifacts:

- `tokenizer.json` — exact 512-token vocabulary and learned BPE merges.
- `history.jsonl` — exact metrics for all 10 epochs.
- `translations.jsonl` — three deterministic validation samples per epoch.
- `loss_curve.svg` — train/validation loss curve generated by the TensorFlow CLI.
