# Day 20 — Extended PyTorch CUDA run

This directory preserves the lightweight, reviewable outputs from the verified
Day 20 continuation. Large model and optimizer checkpoints remain under
`checkpoints/day19/torch/` and are intentionally ignored by Git.

## Run configuration

- Device: NVIDIA CUDA (Quadro M2000M)
- Dataset: first 2,000 aligned Multi30k training pairs and first 200 validation pairs
- Tokenizer: joint English/German BPE, 512 tokens, trained only on the selected training pairs
- Model: 64-dimensional, 4 heads, 2 encoder and decoder layers, 256-dimensional FFN
- Batch size: 64
- Epochs: 40 (resumed at epoch 4 from the verified Day 19 `latest.pt` checkpoint)
- Optimizer steps: 1,280

## Results

| Epoch | Optimizer step | Train loss | Validation loss | Train tokens/s |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 4 | 128 | 4.8897 | 4.9280 | 11,397 |
| 10 | 320 | 4.3800 | 4.4202 | 11,940 |
| 20 | 640 | 3.9018 | 4.0094 | 10,781 |
| 30 | 960 | 3.6813 | 3.8505 | 11,262 |
| 39 | 1,248 | 3.5565 | **3.7800** | 10,958 |
| 40 | 1,280 | 3.5473 | 3.7888 | 11,242 |

The complete run remained finite and stable. Relative to the Day 19 handoff,
training loss improved by 27.5% and best validation loss by 23.3%. Validation
reached its minimum at epoch 39, so `best.pt` contains epoch 39 while
`latest.pt` contains epoch 40.

Greedy samples show some source-dependent variation but are not yet reliable
translations. That limitation is expected from the intentionally small subset,
compact model, and 512-token vocabulary. These samples are diagnostic; formal
BLEU scoring is scheduled for Day 23.

Artifacts:

- `history.jsonl` — exact metrics for all 40 epochs.
- `translations.jsonl` — three deterministic validation samples per epoch.
- `loss_curve.svg` — train/validation loss curve generated by the training CLI.
